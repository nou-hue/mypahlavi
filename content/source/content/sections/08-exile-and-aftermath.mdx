## Departure and death

The shah left Iran on 16 January 1979 [@loc21]. He died in exile in Cairo on 27 July 1980, aged 60, after about four weeks in hospital [@upi1980; @brit-mrp].

## What survives

Several places associated with the dynasty and its predecessors are protected today. Persepolis was inscribed on UNESCO's World Heritage List in 1979 [@unesco114]; the Golestan Palace in 2013 [@unesco1422]; and the Trans-Iranian Railway in 2021 [@unesco1585]. The archive's "Places" collection gives each place card with sources.

## Scope note

This archive ends its narrative in 1980. Events after that date, and current political questions, are deliberately outside its scope.
